End-to-end social media & digital solutions engineered for the UAE market — from strategy and content to paid media and payment integrations.

What We Offer
📱

Social Media Management

We take full ownership of your social presence — crafting a consistent brand voice, publishing on the right platforms at the right times, and building genuine community around your brand across Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, and more.

  • Full-service Instagram, TikTok, Facebook & LinkedIn management
  • Monthly content calendar planning & scheduling
  • Community management & audience engagement
  • Competitor analysis & benchmarking
  • Hashtag research & platform optimisation
  • Dedicated account manager for your brand
🎬

Content Creation

Scroll-stopping content is at the heart of every effective social strategy. Our creative team produces high-quality visuals, Reels, carousels, stories, and copy that authentically express your brand and convert passive viewers into loyal followers and customers.

  • Professional photography & videography direction
  • Reels, TikTok & short-form video production
  • Branded graphic design & carousel posts
  • Arabic & English copywriting & captioning
  • Brand voice development & guidelines
  • Influencer & UGC content collaboration
📣

Paid Advertising

Our paid media specialists build and manage high-performance campaigns across Meta, Google, TikTok, and Snapchat — with deep expertise in the UAE and GCC market. We engineer campaigns for maximum ROAS, brand reach, and lead generation at every budget level.

  • Meta (Facebook & Instagram) Ads management
  • Google Search, Display & YouTube campaigns
  • TikTok & Snapchat paid campaigns
  • A/B testing, creative iteration & optimisation
  • Retargeting & lookalike audience strategies
  • Weekly performance reports & budget pacing

Analytics & Reporting

Data without context is just noise. We deliver clear, actionable monthly reports that translate your social media performance into business insights — showing exactly what's working and where your investment is generating the strongest returns.

  • Monthly branded performance reports
  • Cross-platform analytics dashboard setup
  • Engagement, reach & conversion tracking
  • Paid campaign ROAS & CPA analysis
  • Audience insights & growth trends
  • Strategic recommendations for next period

Payment Gateway Integration

Looking to integrate secure and seamless payment gateways for your online store? Noor Al Bawadi specialises in Stripe, Tabby, Tamara, and more — ensuring smooth transactions and an enhanced shopping experience for your customers.

  • Stripe credit & debit card payment integration
  • Tabby "Buy Now, Pay Later" setup & optimisation
  • Tamara instalment payment integration
  • Secure & hassle-free checkout process design
  • E-commerce platform compatibility (Shopify, WooCommerce, custom)
  • Post-integration testing & ongoing support
