Our Story

Born in Dubai.
Built for Impact.

Noor Al Bawadi Marketing — operating as NBTV Networks — was founded with one unwavering belief: every brand deserves to shine. We started as a creative collective in Dubai's Deira district and grew into a full-service social media marketing agency trusted by over 200 brands across the UAE and the wider Gulf region.

We combine data-driven strategy with bold, culturally intelligent creative work that resonates in the UAE market. Our team understands the nuances of marketing in this region — from multilingual audiences to the explosive influence of local creators — and we use that knowledge to give our clients an edge.

Whether you're a startup launching your first campaign or an established brand looking for a fresh perspective, Noor Al Bawadi Marketing delivers results that matter: real engagement, real growth, and real return on investment.
